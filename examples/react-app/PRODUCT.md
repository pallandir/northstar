# Product

## Audience

A signed in workspace owner at a small or mid sized company using Northwind to track revenue, users and orders.

## Primary job

Compare the three plans, see which one is current, and upgrade or downgrade with confidence.

## Success

The owner finds the right plan in under a minute and reaches the upgrade action without leaving the page to ask what a limit means.

## Constraints

- Lives inside the existing sidebar shell at /pricing
- Works from 360px up
- WCAG 2.2 AA
- Plain CSS, no Tailwind
- Other pages keep their current styles until a later migration
